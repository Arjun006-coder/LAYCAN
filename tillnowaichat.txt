Why "LAYCAN"? (And why judges & industry insiders love it)
In international maritime shipping, LAYCAN stands for:

Laydays / Cancelling — the strict contractual window agreed between the charterer and shipowner during which the vessel must arrive and present ready to load cargo.

If the ship arrives before the laydays, the charterer is not obliged to start loading.
